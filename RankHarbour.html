Common questions

01 Is SEO still relevant with AI search? +

More relevant than ever. AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Gemini all draw from web content to form answers. Businesses with strong organic SEO foundations are the ones appearing in AI results. We specialise in optimising for both traditional Google and AI search simultaneously.

02 How long does SEO take to see results? +

Most clients see meaningful movement in 3–6 months, with compounding growth from months 6–12 onward. The exact timeline depends on your current authority, competition and how quickly technical changes are implemented. We'll give you a data-driven forecast in your free masterplan.

03 Can you guarantee a specific ranking on Google? +

No — and anyone who does is misleading you. Google's algorithm involves hundreds of signals and no agency controls it. What we commit to is transparent, best-practice work, rigorous execution and accountability to real business metrics — not vanity rankings.

04 Do you have lock-in contracts? +

No lock-in contracts. We operate on rolling monthly agreements. Our client retention reflects results, not legal traps — clients stay because the work is producing outcomes, not because they're contractually obligated to.

05 How do you measure and report on success? +

We measure what matters: revenue from organic, qualified leads, keyword rankings, organic traffic growth and AI search visibility. Every monthly report is tied directly to business outcomes. We don't hide behind vanity metrics or keyword lists disconnected from commercial value.
