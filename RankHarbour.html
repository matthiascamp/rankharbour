Good questions

Before you
get started.

What does the website evaluation check?

It reviews the source HTML of one public page against 12 on-page checks, including titles, descriptions, headings, links and images. It provides recommendations, not a full site crawl or a ranking forecast.

Which plan should I choose?

Starter covers the basics. Growth adds ongoing optimisation. Pro adds more page work and a written article each month. Enterprise plans increase content, strategy and support. Compare every inclusion in Plans & Pricing.

How does billing work?

Plans renew every 28 days through Stripe. View your subscription, invoices and cancellation options in Account centre. Google Business Posts is a separate subscription and is managed separately.

Can you guarantee rankings?

No. Search positions depend on many factors. The service focuses on improving your website’s technical foundations, relevance and content, with clear reporting on the work and available performance data.
