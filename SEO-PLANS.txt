# How Our SEO Process Works

SEO is treated as an ongoing cycle rather than a one-time setup.

Each month, we:

1. Analyse website performance and search data
2. Identify new search opportunities
3. Monitor rising and declining search queries
4. Review competitors
5. Identify pages with strong impressions but weak click-through rates
6. Identify keywords close to stronger ranking positions
7. Prioritise the opportunities most likely to generate relevant traffic
8. Optimise existing pages
9. Improve titles, headings, metadata, content, and internal links
10. Create or recommend new search-targeted content
11. Monitor technical SEO issues
12. Measure the impact of previous changes
13. Adjust the SEO strategy for the following month

This creates a continuous improvement cycle:

**Research → Optimise → Publish → Measure → Improve**

# Our Approach to Keywords

We do not simply select a fixed group of keywords when you sign up and leave them unchanged.

Search behaviour constantly changes.

Customers use different phrases, competitors target new searches, seasonal demand changes, and new opportunities appear over time.

Our Continuous Search Opportunity Optimisation process monitors these changes and continually refines your SEO strategy.

This can include:

- Targeting newly emerging searches
- Expanding successful keyword groups
- Improving pages already beginning to rank
- Updating pages that are losing visibility
- Targeting valuable competitor keyword gaps
- Adjusting targeting around seasonal demand
- Creating new articles around valuable customer questions
- Strengthening service pages using supporting content
- Removing or consolidating competing content
- Improving search-result click-through rates

The objective is to continually expand the number of relevant searches through which potential customers can discover your business.

# SEO Content Strategy

Content is created around real search opportunities rather than publishing articles simply for the sake of having a blog.

A typical content strategy may include:

- Customer questions
- Buying guides
- Service explanations
- Product comparisons
- Industry-specific guides
- Local search content
- Educational resources
- Frequently searched problems
- Commercial research queries
- Informational searches that naturally lead toward your services

Every piece of content should have a clear purpose.

Where appropriate, articles are internally linked toward relevant commercial pages so traffic can move from:

**Google Search → Helpful Content → Product or Service Page → Enquiry or Sale**

# What We Measure

SEO performance is measured using meaningful search and website data, including:

- Organic website visitors
- Google Search clicks
- Search impressions
- Click-through rate
- Search queries
- Landing-page performance
- New keyword opportunities
- Keyword visibility trends
- Website engagement
- Enquiries or conversions where tracking is available
- Pages gaining visibility
- Pages losing visibility
- Content performance

We focus on long-term organic growth rather than guaranteeing individual rankings.

No legitimate SEO provider can guarantee a specific Google ranking, position, traffic level, or number of enquiries.

Instead, our goal is to consistently improve the quality, relevance, visibility, and search performance of your website over time.
