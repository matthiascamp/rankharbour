What is SEO and why does my business need it?+
SEO is the practice of improving your website's visibility in organic (unpaid) search results. It's the highest-ROI digital channel for most businesses because traffic compounds — you pay once to rank, then collect traffic for months or years. Paid ads stop the moment you stop paying.
Is SEO still relevant with AI search?+
More relevant than ever. AI Overviews, ChatGPT, and Gemini all draw from web content to form answers. Businesses with strong organic SEO foundations are the ones appearing in AI results. We specialise in optimising for both traditional Google and AI search simultaneously.
How long does SEO take to see results?+
Most clients see meaningful movement in 3–6 months, with compounding growth from months 6–12 onward. The exact timeline depends on your current authority, competition, and how quickly changes are implemented. We'll give you a data-driven forecast after your free audit.
Can you guarantee a #1 ranking on Google?+
No — and anyone who does is lying to you. Google's algorithm involves hundreds of signals and no agency controls it. What we guarantee is transparent, best-practice work, rigorous execution, and accountability to real business metrics.
Do you have lock-in contracts?+
No lock-in contracts. We operate on rolling monthly agreements. Our client retention rate is 98% — not because we trap clients, but because we deliver results every single month.
How much does SEO cost?+
Campaigns start from $2,500/month for smaller businesses and scale for enterprise. The right investment depends on your goals, competitive landscape, and current baseline. We'll recommend a budget during your free masterplan that reflects honest, realistic ROI.
What's included in your SEO service?+
Technical SEO, keyword strategy, content planning, on-page optimisation, link building, digital PR, AI visibility optimisation, monthly reporting, and a dedicated senior specialist — not a junior who reads from a playbook.
How do you measure SEO success?+
We measure what matters: revenue from organic, qualified leads, keyword rankings, organic traffic growth, and AI search visibility. We don't hide behind vanity metrics. Every report is tied directly to business outcomes.
Can you help with a brand new website?+
Absolutely. Starting SEO from day one means we build authority the right way — no bad habits to undo. We work alongside your dev team to ensure your site launches with clean technical SEO from the start.
How often will we communicate?+
Monthly executive reports, weekly check-in calls, and a live dashboard so you always know what's happening. Your senior specialist is reachable directly — not through a ticketing system.
